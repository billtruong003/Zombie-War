# Drop pipeline (G3.3) — 2026-10-04

Why the QA playthrough saw no chest and no mechanic item in ~9 minutes.

| Source | Mechanic item (Magnet/Bomb/Freeze) | Chest |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary enemy | never | never |
| Elite kill | 15 % × Luck (only if none on the map) | 4 % × Luck |
| Supply Drop station | always one (if none on the map) | 20 % × Luck |
| Boss Beacon boss | — | always — **was broken** (paid on a later kill, at that zombie's spot, or never); fixed in G3 |
| Evolution pity | — | next elite guaranteed once an evolution has waited 45 s |

Observed in a fresh Map_Level1 run at ~1 min: 45 alive, 1 elite; 2 Supply Drops, 1 Boss Beacon, 4 Signal Relays streamed in.

Reading: items and chests are gated on elites and on walking to stations. Early in a run elites are rare, so
a player who does not visit a Supply Drop or a Boss Beacon sees almost nothing — and the beacon's chest was
broken. No tuning changed; this is input for the balance phase (owner decision).
